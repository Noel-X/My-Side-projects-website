Time-Loop Sokoban Demo(C++)

Cooperating with the shadow of your past

A classic puzzle game built with C++ and the EasyX graphics library. To ensure a smooth user experience, I used a modular design that decouples the UI display from the core game logic.

In this game, it is actually impossible to reach the goal within the move limit on your own. However, the game features a unique "Time-Loop" mechanic. After your first failed attempt, you start over, but this time a "blue cat shadow" appears on the board. That is your past self—it will repeat every move you made in the previous round. By coordinating with your past actions, you can solve the puzzle together.

"While we are often held back by objective limits, a time loop gives us a second chance—the chance to cooperate with our past selves."

Tech Stack: C++, EasyX Graphics, STL (Stack/Vector)
